About the Project

This project is my proposal to address a challenge posed in Singapore Airlines App Challenge 2023. The issue was two-fold:

  1. The traditional order taking process was time-consuming
  2. The above process also did not promote spontaneous purchases and upselling opportunities

To scope the problem to be feasible at a solo developer level, I focused on the second issue.

When reviewing screenshots of the Scoothub app, I observed that the UI was simple and utilitarian. While this enables customers to find functions easily, it does not convey excitement about the offerings. The items were also displayed by category, requiring customers to sift through to discover.

Image credits: From Google

My solution to increase customer's motivation in exploring inflight offerings is Scootaway Deals, a module to administer flash sale campaigns during flight time. The solution includes:

Consumer-facing features, for customer discovery

  1. Scootaway Deals module
  2. Scootaway Deals Landing Page

Admin Portal features, for campaign management

  1. View, create, update, delete campaigns
  2. Add, remove items to campaigns

Existing pages were also redesigned to incorporate the Scootaway Deals module seamlessly.
