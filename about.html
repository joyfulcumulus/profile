About Me

I started my career at Changi Airport Group, where I worked on analytics solutions for airport operations. Working with vendors, I finetuned computer vision and queue analytics algorithms, enabling accurate data collection to optimise wait times and improve operational efficiencies.

Later, I moved to Shopee (Southeast Asia's leading e-commerce platform) as a product manager, where I led the development of digital products in close collaboration with engineering and data science teams. These included machine learning-powered product features that enhanced user experience, optimised operations and digitalised marketing processes.

Through these experiences, I saw firsthand how data could transform operations and decision-making. As advancements in AI began reshaping industries, I made a deliberate move to deepen my technical capabilities, starting with a coding bootcamp, where I built a strong foundation in programming and developed end-to-end projects as a solo developer.

Building on this foundation, I went on to pursue the SMU Master of IT in Business (MITB), specialising in Data Science & Analytics, to further develop my expertise in machine learning, data engineering, and AI.

I am now looking to bring together my business acumen, product experience, and technical expertise to drive impactful, data-driven solutions. Feel free to connect with me on LinkedIn!

What I Bring

  1. Applied Machine Learning & Analytics: Experience building ML models, agentic AI systems, and data pipelines on large, complex datasets to generate actionable insights.
  2. Product-Driven Problem Solving: 8 years in product and operations, translating business problems into long-term capabilities.
  3. Business Impact: Proven ability to deliver measurable outcomes by optimising operations and driving efficiency at scale.
