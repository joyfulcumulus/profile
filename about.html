About Me

I started my career as an operations professional setting up sensors and analytics solutions. As the end user, I finetuned computer vision and queue analytics algorithms with vendors, gaining experience on how to leverage technology for businesses. I enjoyed seeing data-driven insights translate to meaningful outcomes.

To gain further expertise in tech, I joined Shopee, Southeast Asia's e-commerce giant, where I thrived as a product manager. I introduced new data science services, designed improved UI/UX flows and managed big data, in the domains of operations, user account and marketing across 16 markets. I gained data science knowledge, skills in UI/UX and system design, and became proficient in tools like Confluence and JIRA.

The above experiences sparked in me a desire to create tech and data products. I felt that it would be empowering to be able to derive insights and bring ideas to reality. As such, I enrolled in a coding bootcamp to gain fundamental skills in programming, after which I proceeded to complete personal projects to develop my skills in both web development and data analytics further.
